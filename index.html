Smith chart in plain language

How to read the movement

1. The circle. The load starts on a constant-VSWR circle. Moving toward the generator rotates around that circle; the amount of reflection stays the same.

2. The centre. The centre is a perfect match: no reflected wave, normalized impedance z = 1 + j0.

3. The crossing. A shunt stub works where the conductance is already g = 1. The stub then cancels the remaining susceptance and moves the point to the centre.
